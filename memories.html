Seven reasons I love you

Every picture has a story. These are just a few of the reasons you mean so much to me.

Reason 01

Your Smile

I love your smile because it can change the whole mood of a moment. Seeing you genuinely happy will always be one of my favorite things.

Reason 02

The Little Things

The little gifts, the spontaneous moments, and the ordinary days that turn into memories. I notice more than you know, and every small thing adds another page to our story.

Reason 03

Your Energy

You bring this blooming aura wherever you go. You can be loud, full of life, and impossible not to notice. There’s something about your energy that makes even an ordinary room feel brighter.

Reason 04

Your Beauty

There are so many sides of you, and I love getting to see them all. Whether you’re posing for a picture or just enjoying a quiet moment, I find something new to admire every time.

Reason 05

Our Memories

From our random pictures to the days we spent together, these are the moments I replay in my head. I love that we have our own stories behind every photo.

Reason 06

The Way You Care

I love the way you care about me. It’s in the little messages, checking on me, wishing me a good day, and reminding me that I’m loved. Even with the distance between us, you still find ways to make me feel cared for.

Reason 07

Us

Out of everything on this page, this might be my favorite part. Not because every moment is perfect, but because they’re ours. The random pictures, the blurry ones, the gifts, the laughs, and the moments where it’s just me and you being us. I hope we get to make a whole lot more of them.
