To my Dory ♡

Sometimes I wish distance could be measured in steps instead of miles. It would make it a lot easier to get to you.

But even with the distance, you've become such a meaningful part of my life. These pictures are little reminders of the laughs, the random moments, the memories, and the person behind all of them.

I don't want this website to be just a gift. I want it to be something we can look back at later and say, “Look how far we've come.”

There are still so many pictures we haven't taken, places we haven't gone, dates we haven't had, and memories we haven't made.

So this isn't the end of our story. It's just one little page in it.

I love you, Dory. Always remember that. ♡

— Rolph
